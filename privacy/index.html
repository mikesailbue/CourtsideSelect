Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Courtside Select is a basketball highlight-recording app provided by Michael Calkins. This policy explains how the app handles your information and how to contact us.

Recordings and game information

The app processes camera video and microphone audio to record basketball highlights. Video clips, game and team names, scores, game dates, clip selections, and trim points are stored on your device. Recording preferences are also stored locally. The app uses temporary files to buffer footage and assemble highlights and exported reels.

Data collection and tracking

The app does not require an account and does not upload recordings or game information to servers operated by the developer. It does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. The developer does not receive your recordings through ordinary use of the app.

Permissions

The app requests camera and microphone access for recording video with sound. You can manage these permissions in iPhone Settings. If you choose to save or share an export through Photos, Files, or another app, iOS manages that action and any permissions it requires.

Sharing and device backups

Recordings leave the app when you choose to share or export them. The receiving service or app handles those copies under its own privacy practices. Your device settings may allow app data or exported files to be included in Apple device backups or synced through services such as iCloud. The developer does not operate those services or control copies you share.

Retention and deletion

Saved clips and game information remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app and its data. Switching a clip’s Include setting off does not delete it. Use Delete skipped clips to remove skipped originals, or delete a game and its clips through game setup. Trimming preserves the original video and saves the selected start and end points. Copies exported to Photos, Files, other apps, or backups must be managed separately in those locations.

Support email

If you email calkinsapps@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and any information or attachments you include, for responding to your request. Email is handled by the email providers involved. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information or private footage. You may use this address to request deletion of information you previously sent to support, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Children and recordings of others

The app does not provide child accounts, public profiles, or an in-app public sharing service. Recordings can contain images or voices of other people, including children. Obtain appropriate permission before recording or sharing footage, and follow the rules of the school, team, venue, and applicable law.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and contains no developer-added analytics, advertising, or contact forms. GitHub may process technical information about visits as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Opening a support email link uses your email app.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated to reflect them.
